Health Information

Find important health-related information and tips for a healthy lifestyle.

Walking

Improves cardiovascular health, helps with weight management.

Recommended: 30 minutes daily

Meditation

Reduces stress, improves mental clarity.

Recommended: 10-20 minutes daily

Strength Training

Builds muscle, improves bone density.

Recommended: 2-3 times per week

Sleep

Supports immune system, enhances memory.

Recommended: 7-9 hours nightly

Running

Boosts endurance, burns calories, improves heart health.

Recommended: 20-30 minutes, 3 times per week

Gaming (Pokémon GO / Active Games)

Encourages physical activity, social interaction, cognitive function, and problem-solving skills.

Recommended: 30-60 minutes daily with breaks
