V3 has 83 playable scenes: 65 additions, three revised existing scenes, and 15 retained scenes. Six retained scenes were not mentioned in this attachment. Earlier versions remain unchanged.

- Opening the milk carton from the wrong side is a new action, distinct from milk-first cereal and leaving a teaspoon in a carton.
- Squeezing a filled doughnut is distinct from taking one bite from every doughnut.
- Closing overlapping cupboard doors is distinct from the existing PUSH-door scene.
- The banana, chocolate and burger actions are updated in place, not added as second versions in the v3 collection.
- The banana uses the offered alternative: tearing the peel into small pieces.
