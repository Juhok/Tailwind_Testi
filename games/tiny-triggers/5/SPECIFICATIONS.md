# Volume 5 specifications

25 new scenes extend the unchanged [Volume 4 collection](../4/SPECIFICATIONS.md). All have mouse/touch input and visible intermediate/final outcomes.

1. **The Last Curtain Clip** (`curtain-clip`) — Close both curtains; a clip pops off, the upper corner drops, and daylight leaks through.
2. **The Robot Vacuum and the Rug** (`vacuum-rug`) — Guide the vacuum across the rug, watch it fold a corner, then encounter that corner on its next lap.
3. **The Code Unformatter** (`code-unformatter`) — Format neat code into uneven indents, awkward brackets, and extra spaces; receive Formatting complete.
4. **The Off-Center Chase Card** (`chase-card`) — Tear a booster open and slowly reveal sparkling rare artwork with severely unequal borders.
5. **The Toilet Paper Hunt** (`toilet-hunt`) — Rotate the enclosed jumbo roll, find its end, pull only one or two sheets, then watch the end vanish.
6. **The Armor Paywall** (`armor-paywall`) — Win a battle, earn and equip spectacular armor, then encounter the fictional €12.99 playtime popup before using it.
7. **The Missing Duvet Corner** (`duvet-corner`) — Insert and shake the duvet; three corners fit, the fourth bunches centrally, and correcting it dislodges its opposite.
8. **The Broken Pull Tab** (`pull-tab`) — Lift the can ring and pull; the ring breaks away while the can remains fully sealed.
9. **The Almost-Perfect Puzzle Piece** (`oversized-puzzle`) — Align the matching final piece; its oversized edge resists, and pressing it lifts surrounding pieces.
10. **Cheese-Puff Fingers** (`cheese-fingers`) — Eat cheese puffs, mark mouse and keyboard orange, and release a sticky key that rises slowly.
11. **The Forgotten Milk** (`forgotten-milk`) — Unpack a full grocery bag, prepare coffee and mug, then open the fridge to find no milk.
12. **The Never-Dropping Loot** (`never-loot`) — Defeat 50 enemies with visible progress and unwanted drops; the rare reward belongs to another class.
13. **“Peel Here”** (`peel-here`) — Peel the marked food-package corner; only thin laminate comes away, the actual seal stays intact, and the tab is gone.
14. **The Collapsing Bin Bag** (`collapsing-bag`) — Drop rubbish into a freshly lined bin; its bag rim slips down and the rubbish falls outside the liner.
15. **The Wet Sock** (`wet-sock`) — Walk a clean sock into the only tiny kitchen puddle and watch a wet patch spread.
16. **The Fabric-Eating Zipper** (`fabric-zipper`) — Zip the jacket nearly to the chin; catch the lining, then wedge it tighter by pulling backward.
17. **The Microwave Ice Cube** (`microwave-ice`) — Heat a steaming meal on a visibly hot plate, then cut it open to expose a frozen central chunk.
18. **Water Up Your Sleeve** (`water-sleeve`) — Wash hands and raise them; water travels down the wrist and disappears deep into the sleeve.
19. **Cold Butter, Soft Bread** (`cold-butter`) — Drag a solid cold butter lump over soft bread; it stays intact while tearing a hole in the slice.
20. **One Earbud Gives Up** (`earbud`) — Insert both earbuds and play music in both channels; one reports Battery low and stops, leaving the other playing.
21. **The Extra Printed Page** (`printed-period`) — Print a one-page document, then receive a second sheet containing only its final full stop.
22. **The Cookie Button Switch** (`cookie-switch`) — Aim at Reject All; the fictional banner switches button positions at click, closes, and confirms Preferences saved.
23. **Ten Centimeters Too Short** (`short-cable`) — Plug in the phone and settle on a sofa; the final lean pulls out the too-short charging connector.
24. **The Elevator Door Button** (`elevator`) — Press Close Doors; the doors almost meet, pause, and reopen onto an empty hallway.
25. **The Controller Disconnects** (`controller`) — Reduce a boss to one hit, attack, and lose controller connection while the boss continues preparing its attack.

## Related scenes remain distinct

The new duvet is not the fitted sheet; the oversized matching puzzle piece is not the intentionally misplaced old piece. The new zipper specifically jams harder on reversal. The collapsing bag slips after a correctly aimed drop, unlike deliberately dropping beside the liner. The new cold butter tears plain soft bread, rather than smearing butter over jam. The jumbo-roll hunt is separate from choosing roll orientation. These are different interaction sequences, not duplicate copies of the old scenes.

## Presentation and progression

The 83 original scene identities and chapter order stay unchanged. Four appended chapters group the new domestic, kitchen, digital, and game-related scenes. First-run v4 progress is copied into an independent v5 save without modifying the old one. The collection remains locked until all 108 scenes are complete. Fictional subscriptions and cookie banners never perform real payments, consent changes, or network requests.
