# Volume 8 additions

These are the six ideas reserved as items 21–26 in the v7 request, now authorized for v8.

21. **The Suddenly Busy Crossing.** Walk toward a pedestrian crossing along a quiet road. Just as you reach the curb, a seemingly endless stream of cars arrives from both directions.
22. **The Mystery Hole in the Lid.** Unpack a pudding cup at home and notice a tiny puncture in the foil lid. Tilt it toward the light and discover pudding smeared underneath. Was it already broken at the shop, or did something in your bag pierce it?
23. **The Mostly Empty Nugget Bag.** Open a large bag of nuggets and tip it onto a baking tray. Only a tiny handful falls out. Shake the bag hopefully; one final crumb drops onto the tray.
24. **Perfect Parking, Impossible Exit.** Carefully maneuver into a tight parking space. Open your door, but it moves only a few centimeters before meeting an obstacle. Squeeze through the gap and brush your clean trousers against the dirty car.
25. **The Fogging Windshield.** Set off with a clear windshield. Fog creeps across it until only a tiny clear patch remains. Adjust the ventilation, briefly clear the glass, and watch the fog start returning.
26. **The Gap That Never Comes.** Wait at a give-way sign to join a busy road. Every promising gap closes just as you prepare to move. Eventually, a huge gap appears—but a car from the opposite side takes it first.

No scenes are duplicated or removed. The first 128 identities and chapter positions remain exactly as in v7.
