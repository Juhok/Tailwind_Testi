# V7 requests

Implement items 1–20. The Finnish heading says the final six are reserved for the following update; do not include 21–26 in v7.

1. 🍔 The Endless Ordering Kiosk
   Order one burger at a touchscreen kiosk. Navigate ten screens asking about upgrades, extras, membership, and payment confirmation. Every time you think you are finished, another “Continue” button appears.
2. 💤 The AFK Teammate
   Start a five-player team match. Four players leave the starting area; the fifth stands perfectly still. The match clock fast-forwards to 15 minutes, and they still have not moved.
3. 🎮 Stick Drift
   Release the controller stick, but your character keeps walking forward. Hold the stick slightly backward to stay still. The moment you let go, the character wanders off again.
4. 🌐 Online Required for Offline Mode
   Select “Play Offline.” A loading spinner appears, followed by: “An internet connection is required to access offline mode.” Your only options are “Retry” and “Quit.”
5. 👹 The Entire Dungeon Pull
   Carefully approach a small group of enemies with your party. One teammate runs ahead and attracts every monster in the room. A huge crowd follows them back, overwhelming the entire party.
6. 🧙 The Mage Needs Your Sword
   Defeat a boss and reveal the perfect warrior sword. Before you can claim it, the mage selects “Need” instead of “Greed,” wins the roll, and receives a weapon they cannot even equip.
7. 🔇 The Wrong Audio Output
   Launch a game wearing your headphones. Everything is silent. Open the audio settings and discover that the game has selected an unused monitor speaker instead of your headphones.
8. 🔄 Update and Shut Down
   Finish using your computer and click “Shut Down.” The screen changes to “Installing updates. Do not turn off your computer.” The progress bar reaches 99% and stays there.
9. 💬 Autocorrect Betrayal
   Type “I’ll bring the snacks.” Just as you press Send, autocorrect changes it to “I’ll bring the snakes.” The message sends, and “Read” immediately appears underneath.
10. 👖 The Twisted Trouser Leg
    Put on your trousers. One foot slides through easily, but the other gets stuck halfway down a twisted leg. Take them off, untwist the fabric, and start again.
11. 💡 The Unreliable Light Switch
    Flip a light switch. The bulb flashes briefly, then goes dark. Nudge the switch into a very specific position and the light works—until you take your finger away.
12. 🔋 The Wrong Travel Plug
    Arrive at your hotel with 1% phone battery. Unpack your charger and move it toward the wall socket. The plug does not fit, no matter which way you rotate it.
13. 🚦 Red Lights at 3 AM
    Drive along a completely empty road at three in the morning. As you approach the intersection, the lights turn red. Wait while every other direction gets a green light for absolutely nobody.
14. 🛒 The Checkout Ambush
    Spot an empty checkout and walk toward it with your shopping. Just before you arrive, five customers converge from different aisles and join the queue ahead of you.
15. 🪙 Exact Change, Eventually
    Wait behind one customer at the checkout. They slowly place tiny coins on the counter, one at a time. After counting them all, they are one coin short and start searching another pocket.
16. 🏷️ Everything Except Your Product
    Find the correct supermarket aisle and scan a fully stocked shelf. Every flavor and variation is available except the exact one you came to buy. Its price label sits beneath an empty space.
17. 🌾 The Leaking Flour Bag
    Pay for a bag of flour and pick it up from the checkout. A tiny hole in the bottom leaves a white trail behind you. Turn the bag to inspect it, and flour spills onto your clothes.
18. 🥤 The Fizzy Bottle Surprise
    Bring home a bottle of soda and slowly loosen the cap. A small hiss suddenly becomes a fountain. Try to screw the cap back on while foam spills over your hands.
19. 🚘 The Wrong Wiper Setting
    Try to switch on the front wipers. Move the stalk the wrong way and spray washer fluid across the windshield instead. The rear wiper also starts dragging noisily across dry glass.
20. 🚗 Third Gear at the Traffic Lights
    Wait through a long red light, forgetting the car is still in third gear. Green appears, you try to move, and the engine stalls. Cars honk behind you. By the time you restart, the light is red again.
