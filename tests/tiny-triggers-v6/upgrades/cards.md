# One face up

Patterned physical deck on green felt, with layered edges/shadows, repeating diamond backs and hand-drawn ace of hearts face. Pull the loose card across to roll it edge-on and face up, then slide it into the deck. A smooth fan/shuffle leaves the face-up ace conspicuous among back-facing neighbors. Deck dragging cannot skip the reveal.
