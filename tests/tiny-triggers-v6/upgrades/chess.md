# Black on the right

A carved, wood-grained chessboard with alternating shaded ivory/slate squares, coordinates, and individually illustrated pawns, rooks and kings. Dragging the rim rotates the complete board and pieces continuously, followed by a weighted quarter-turn settle. The bottom-right square really changes from ivory to black. Center/piece grabs do not turn it; short turns spring back. Active cancellation returns an uncommitted turn without undoing completed results.
