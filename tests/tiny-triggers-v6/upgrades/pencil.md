# A perfect point, briefly

Electric sharpener with metal port, indicator and transparent shavings tray; faceted pencil with ferrule/eraser, wood cone and real graphite tip. Hold the pencil in the sharpener to form a point and accumulate curled shavings, then press that point onto ruled paper. Graphite snaps, flies aside and leaves a dark mark; unsharpened pressing does not finish.
