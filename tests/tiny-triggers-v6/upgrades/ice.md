# The chosen cube

Six translucent faceted ice cubes in recessed blue tray wells; the wanted cube has a gold-marked well. Cubes can be individually lifted to a waiting bowl, casting moving shadows. Pulling the selected cube instead flexes/shudders it without releasing. Completion requires freeing every other cube plus attempting the stuck one, leaving exactly one cube.
