# Crumbs on top

Crumpled printed crisp bag with sealed top, tearable bottom strip and ragged dark opening. Tear along the bottom seam, then lift/tilt the bag over a bowl of ridged whole crisps. Animated crumbs fall from the moving opening and accumulate on top; detached seam stays on tabletop rather than floating with the bag. Bounded crumb field remains after completion.
